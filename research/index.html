02 · Current investigations

Questions under examination

  • XR and reflective environments: mirror optics, two-way mirror displays, augmented-reality mirrors and spatially extended interaction.
  • Embodied interaction: movement traces, reflected-body interaction, choreography and relationships between physical and computational representation.
  • Reflective computing: personal observation, context, journaling and interfaces that support reflection without judging or scoring people.
  • Technological independence: local-first GNU/Linux systems, reproducible environments, libre software and human-controlled infrastructure.
  • African-centred knowledge: historical inquiry, philosophy, language, cultural memory, attribution and the stewardship of knowledge.

These are research directions, not claims that every capability has been implemented or validated.

04 · Methods and principles

Evidence before claims

  • Distinguish published evidence, founder interpretation, hypothesis, proposal, implementation and verified result.
  • Attribute ideas, quotations, research and creative contributions to their authors.
  • Preserve source links, dates, versions and relevant limitations.
  • Prefer reproducible technical work and documented experiments.
  • Respect privacy, consent, local control and data minimisation.
  • Do not turn people into scores, hidden profiles or diagnostic outputs.
  • Record negative results and unresolved questions, not only successful demonstrations.

05 · Reading project status

What each status means

Project labels describe the maturity of a particular artefact or claim, not the overall maturity of an entire research domain.

LIVE

Available and in use in the stated context. Availability does not imply universal support.

PROTOTYPE

An implemented experiment for evaluation; not necessarily stable or suitable for general use.

RESEARCH

An active investigation, literature review or technical exploration without a released product claim.

PROPOSAL

A documented possibility or design direction that has not yet been implemented.

When a status is unknown or evidence is incomplete, it should remain explicitly unclassified rather than being promoted to LIVE.
