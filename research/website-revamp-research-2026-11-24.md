# Website Revamp Research — NAKEBUSEN · WARENA POWERED

**Research date:** 24 November 2026  
**Scope:** Public-facing positioning, Eldoret loc-care market signals, local-service conversion, search presentation and accessibility  
**Status:** Directional desk research; not a customer study, legal opinion or conversion measurement

## Executive recommendation

Keep **Beautiful Roots. Sovereign Systems.** as the master positioning. It joins NAKEBUSEN's beauty, heritage and craft with WARENA's systems capability. Keep **Freedom Is a System.** as WARENA's technical line. **Sovereign Futures** is useful supporting horizon language, but it is less concrete as the primary line and is already used as the name of an existing talent network; this is a distinctiveness observation, not a finding of trademark conflict.

The homepage should explain the actual offer before asking visitors to interpret the full brand architecture:

1. Professional loc care in Eldoret.
2. Practical computing and network support through WARENA.
3. A specific loc-care enquiry action and a clear route to published service prices.
4. About, IKAI research, GENJE creative practice and FELOH Corpus as the deeper practice layer.

## 1. Local market signals

A dated public search surfaced multiple loc-focused providers in Eldoret, including ADECARGO DREADLOCKS / ADECARGO LOCS & BEAUTY, Flo Sisterlocks, Alif Locs Eldoret, Paul Dreadlocks and other Oloo Street and town-centre providers. These listings indicate that loc services are an established visible category in the local online market. Directory listings and review counts are snapshots and may change; they are not independent audits of service quality.

Two local websites provide useful public-facing comparators:

- [KJ Salon — Natural Hair Services](https://kjsalon.co.ke/services/natural-hair) combines a service menu, Eldoret location details, appointment requests and WhatsApp contact. Its public page describes loc and Sisterlocks services within a broader salon offer.
- [Eldoret's Finest Hair Salon — Styles](https://afrohairsalon-eldoret.com/styles/) presents loc services, published price ranges and WhatsApp booking. Its prices and service definitions are not directly comparable to NAKEBUSEN's approved service catalogue.

### What this means for NAKEBUSEN

- Show the three approved services and exact published prices clearly.
- Make the Eldoret location and map/directions easy to find.
- Keep a direct WhatsApp enquiry path, but do not imply instant confirmation or an automated booking.
- State the service boundary plainly: no braiding or shaving; the founder does not cut clients' natural hair.
- Do not compete through unsupported “best”, “expert”, “certified”, review or outcome claims.
- Do not copy a competitor's service list or pricing. The service catalogue remains the authority.

The exact phrase choice should be revisited only through a deliberate brand-canon update, not silently changed in homepage copy. KIPI describes slogans as possible marks and provides Form TM27 for a search / preliminary advice on distinctiveness. A web search is not a trademark clearance search.

## 3. Local search and page metadata

Google Search Central says title links may draw from the document title, visible page title, headings, prominent text, social metadata and other sources. Its guidance favours descriptive, concise titles and discourages repetitive keyword stuffing.

For this single-page site, the selected title is:

**NAKEBUSEN | Loc Care in Eldoret · WARENA Powered**

The description leads with loc care, the locality and the direct enquiry action. Technical capability remains in the page content and supporting social description.

## 4. Accessibility and mobile experience

The current static architecture is appropriate to a lightweight, mobile-first service page: it avoids remote fonts, third-party scripts and unnecessary runtime dependencies. Keep both languages available through native controls, preserve semantic landmarks and maintain a visible direct contact path.

Acceptance targets:
- Reflow at 320 CSS pixels without horizontal scrolling.
- Keep primary interactive targets at least 44 × 44 CSS pixels as the site's design target; WCAG 2.2 AA's minimum target-size criterion is 24 × 24 CSS pixels subject to its exceptions.
- Ensure fixed navigation does not obscure focused elements or anchor destinations.
- Verify light/dark appearance, keyboard navigation, focus visibility, reduced motion and reduced transparency.
- Check actual rendered contrast, particularly on translucent hero/navigation surfaces.

References:
- [W3C — WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/)
- [W3C WAI — What's New in WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/WAI/standards-guidelines/wcag/new-in-22/)

## 5. Research limitations and next validation

This desk research does not establish NAKEBUSEN's customer demand, conversion rate, market share or customer satisfaction; competitor quality or exact service equivalence; visitor understanding of the brand line; trademark availability; or actual Core Web Vitals and screen-reader conformance.

Next validation should use real enquiries and voluntary customer interviews, without adding analytics or tracking unless separately approved. Record enquiry source, requested service, questions asked and confirmed outcomes in a privacy-conscious operational record. Do not publish customer stories or photographs without explicit permission.
