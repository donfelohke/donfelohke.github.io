# Code of Conduct

We aim for respectful, practical and accountable collaboration.

Expected: respect, specific feedback, cultural awareness, attribution, privacy protection, honest uncertainty and critique of ideas rather than people.

Unacceptable: harassment, doxxing, threats, discriminatory abuse, malicious disclosure of private information or repeated disruptive conduct.

Report concerns privately to the repository maintainer through a trusted contact route. Do not post sensitive incident details in public issues. Assess reports fairly, protect affected people and act proportionately.
