# Code of Conduct

We aim for respectful, practical and accountable collaboration. Expected behaviour includes respectful, specific feedback, cultural awareness, attribution, privacy protection, honest uncertainty and critique of ideas rather than people.

Harassment, doxxing, threats, discriminatory abuse, malicious disclosure of private information and repeated disruptive conduct are unacceptable. Report concerns privately to the repository maintainer through a trusted contact route. Do not post sensitive incident details in public issues. Assess reports fairly, protect affected people and act proportionately.
