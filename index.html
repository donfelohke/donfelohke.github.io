05 / IKAI · MIRROR05 / IKAI · KIOO

A mirror,
not a judge.

RESEARCH · WORK IN PROGRESS

Kioo,
si hakimu.

UTAFITI · UNAENDELEA

IKAI investigates reflective computing: the integration of reflective optics, sensing, displays, local computation and human-directed interaction in physical environments. “IKAI” is a Turkana female name meaning “home”.

The mirror is an initial experimental embodiment, not the limit of the research. Any future sensing or collection must be transparent, consent-based, purpose-limited and documented. IKAI must not identify, rank, diagnose, covertly monitor or secretly profile people.

IKAI huchunguza mifumo ya kompyuta ya uakisi: kuunganisha uakisi wa macho, vihisi, skrini, kompyuta za ndani na mwingiliano unaoongozwa na binadamu katika mazingira halisi. “IKAI” ni jina la kike la Kiturkana linalomaanisha “nyumbani”.

Kioo ni mwanzo wa majaribio, si mpaka wa utafiti. Ukusanyaji wowote wa taarifa baadaye lazima uwe wazi, kwa ridhaa, kwa madhumuni maalumu na uandikwe. IKAI haitatumika kuwatambua watu, kuwapanga kwa viwango, kuwachunguza, kuwafuatilia au kuwaprofaili kwa siri.

06 / GENJE · EXPRESSION06 / GENJE · UJIELEZAJI

Rhythm made
embodied.

CREATIVE PRACTICE

Midundo inayodhihirika
kwa mwili.

SHUGHULI YA UBUNIFU

GENJE is a Genge-rooted creative practice where sound, code, rhythm, movement and the body meet. It explores livecoding, audiovisual experimentation, choreography, improvisation, movement notation and choreohacking.

Technology serves the creative work; it does not replace the human author, dancer or practitioner.

GENJE ni shughuli ya ubunifu yenye mizizi ya Genge, ambapo sauti, msimbo, midundo, mwendo na mwili hukutana. Inachunguza livecoding, majaribio ya sauti na picha, choreografia, ubunifu wa papo hapo, uandishi wa mwendo na choreohacking.

Teknolojia hutumikia kazi ya ubunifu; haichukui nafasi ya mwandishi, mcheza dansi au mtendaji.
