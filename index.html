04 / IKAI · MIRROR

Home, reflected.

RESEARCH · WORK IN PROGRESS · NOT A RELEASED PRODUCT

A mirror, not a judge.

IKAI is a Turkana female name meaning “home.” It remains research / WIP, not a released product. It investigates reflective computing: the integration of reflective optics, sensing, displays, local computation and human-directed interaction in physical environments.

Research directions include two-way mirror displays, transparent displays, digital and virtual mirrors, spatial interfaces and real-time bidirectional input/output. The mirror is an initial experimental embodiment, not the limit of the research.

IKAI must not identify, rank, diagnose, covertly monitor or secretly profile people. Any future sensing or collection must be transparent, consent-based, purpose-limited and documented.

05 / GENJE · EXPRESSION

Rhythm made embodied.

CREATIVE PRACTICE · RESEARCH + WORKS

Sound. Movement. Code. Livity.

GENJE is a Genge-rooted creative practice where music, code, rhythm, movement and the body meet. It explores Genge, electronic African club music, neo-soul, reggae and dancehall influences, algorithmic composition, livecoding and performance.

Its practice extends into choreography, improvisation, choreohacking, movement notation and embodied knowledge. Technology serves the creative work; it does not replace the human author, dancer or practitioner.

04 / IKAI · KIOO

Nyumbani, kwa taswira.

UTAFITI · UNAENDELEA · SI BIDHAA ILIYOTOLEWA

Kioo, si hakimu.

IKAI ni jina la kike la Kiturkana linalomaanisha “nyumbani”. Linachunguza kompyuta za kutafakari: matumizi ya vioo, mwanga, vihisi, skrini, kompyuta za ndani na mwingiliano unaoongozwa na binadamu katika mazingira halisi.

Maeneo ya utafiti yanajumuisha skrini za kioo cha njia mbili, skrini angavu, vioo vya kidijitali, violesura vya anga na ingizo/tokeo la pande mbili kwa wakati halisi.

IKAI haipaswi kuwatambua, kuwapanga kwa alama, kuwachunguza kiafya au kuwafuatilia watu kwa siri. Ukusanyaji wowote wa baadaye lazima uwe wazi, kwa ridhaa na wenye madhumuni yaliyoelezwa.
