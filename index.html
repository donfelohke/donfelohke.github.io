GRACE · GRIT · GLORY

I am Kenyan. I’m Felix Lwova, known as Don Feloh.

I’m a Kenyan founder, systems practitioner, loctician and creative technologist. My work moves between community-based physical care, GNU/Linux systems, reflective-computing research, sound and movement. NAKEBUSEN is the grounded enterprise; WARENA develops the technical capability; GENJE carries creative expression; IKAI holds the reflective-computing inquiry.

My practice is guided by human authorship, local-first infrastructure, craft, cultural continuity and the belief that technology should extend human agency rather than replace it. AI may suggest; the human decides.

These are connected practices, not one undifferentiated company. NAKEBUSEN is the only commercial transaction point. WARENA supplies capability; IKAI remains research; GENJE remains creative practice. The FELOH Corpus records shared decisions, sources and evidence.

NAKEBUSEN began in Lodwar, Turkana County, Kenya; its loc-care practice continues in Eldoret.

05 / IKAI · MIRROR05 / IKAI · KIOO

A mirror,
not a judge.

RESEARCH · WORK IN PROGRESS

Kioo,
si hakimu.

UTAFITI · UNAENDELEA

IKAI investigates reflective computing: the integration of reflective optics, sensing, displays, local computation and human-directed interaction in physical environments. IKAI is a Turkana female name meaning “home”. This is the founder-approved identity meaning; it is not a claim of independent linguistic certification or independently documented community usage.

The mirror is an initial experimental embodiment, not the limit of the research. Any future sensing or collection must be transparent, consent-based, purpose-limited and documented. IKAI must not identify, rank, diagnose, covertly monitor or secretly profile people.

IKAI huchunguza mifumo ya kompyuta ya uakisi: kuunganisha uakisi wa macho, vihisi, skrini, kompyuta za ndani na mwingiliano unaoongozwa na binadamu katika mazingira halisi. IKAI ni jina la kike la Kiturkana lenye maana ya “nyumbani”. Hii ndiyo maana ya utambulisho iliyoidhinishwa na mwanzilishi; si dai la uthibitisho huru wa kiisimu au matumizi ya jamii yaliyothibitishwa kwa kujitegemea.

Kioo ni mwanzo wa majaribio, si mpaka wa utafiti. Ukusanyaji wowote wa taarifa baadaye lazima uwe wazi, kwa ridhaa, kwa madhumuni maalumu na uandikwe. IKAI haitatumika kuwatambua watu, kuwapanga kwa viwango, kuwachunguza, kuwafuatilia au kuwaprofaili kwa siri.

06 / GENJE · EXPRESSION06 / GENJE · UJIELEZAJI

Rhythm made
embodied.

CREATIVE PRACTICE

Midundo inayodhihirika
kwa mwili.

SHUGHULI YA UBUNIFU

GENJE is a Genge-rooted creative practice where sound, code, rhythm, movement and the body meet. It explores E-Genge, livecoding, audiovisual experimentation, algorithmic improvisation, choreography, movement notation, Laban-informed analysis and choreohacking.

Technology serves the creative work; it does not replace the human author, dancer or practitioner.

GENJE ni shughuli ya ubunifu yenye mizizi ya Genge, ambapo sauti, msimbo, midundo, mwendo na mwili hukutana. Inachunguza E-Genge, livecoding, majaribio ya sauti na picha, ubunifu wa algoriti wa papo hapo, choreografia, uchambuzi wa mwendo unaotumia misingi ya Laban na choreohacking.

Teknolojia hutumikia kazi ya ubunifu; haichukui nafasi ya mwandishi, mcheza dansi au mtendaji.
