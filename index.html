GRACE · GRIT · GLORY

I’m Felix Lwova, known as Don Feloh. I founded NAKEBUSEN and WARENA, and work across loc care, GNU/Linux systems and creative technology.

My practice is grounded in craft, human authorship and technology that strengthens human agency. NAKEBUSEN began in Lodwar, Turkana County, Kenya; its loc-care practice continues in Eldoret.

Mimi ni Felix Lwova, ninayejulikana kama Don Feloh. Nilianzisha NAKEBUSEN na WARENA; kazi yangu inahusisha utunzaji wa loc, mifumo ya GNU/Linux na teknolojia ya ubunifu.

Shughuli zangu huongozwa na ufundi, ubunifu unaoongozwa na binadamu na teknolojia inayopanua uwezo wa binadamu. NAKEBUSEN ilianza Lodwar, Kaunti ya Turkana, Kenya; huduma zake za utunzaji wa loc zinaendelea Eldoret.
