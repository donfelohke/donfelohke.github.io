03 — CODE | GRIT

NAKEBUSEN DIGITAL

Powered by WARENA

Freedom Is a System.

Technology should be understandable, maintainable and useful beyond the moment it is installed.

NAKEBUSEN DIGITAL provides practical technical services through the WARENA systems practice, with an emphasis on GNU/Linux, local-first computing, networking, system administration and reproducible environments.

  • GNU/Linux installation, administration and troubleshooting
  • Computer repair, recovery and system maintenance
  • Network configuration, Wi-Fi and local-area networking
  • Router, server and small-office infrastructure
  • Laboratory, classroom and cybercafé systems
  • Offline-capable and local-first computing environments
  • Reproducible development environments and systems engineering

Every engagement starts with the actual problem, equipment, constraints and intended outcome. Scope, feasibility, pricing and support are agreed before work begins.

IKAI — Reflective Compute

IKAI is a research and development initiative exploring reflective, local-first computing. Its guiding question is not how to make technology judge people, but how to make systems more useful for human reflection, understanding and agency.

IKAI remains a work in progress.
