Ideas need room
to become.

Mawazo yanahitaji nafasi
ya kukua.

This is the editorial space for worldview, philosophy, thought, opinion and reflection. It holds questions, developing ideas and considered positions without pretending they are all the same kind of statement.

  • Worldview — African consciousness, heritage, sovereignty and futures.
  • Philosophy — freedom, ethics, knowledge, dignity and responsibility.
  • Thought & ideas — questions, hypotheses and developing frameworks.
  • Opinion — society, political economy, institutions and accountability.
  • Reflection — identity, memory, experience and becoming.
  • Influences — thinkers, books, traditions, disciplines, music and technology.

A question is not a conclusion. A published position should carry its date, context and source.

Hapa ndipo nafasi ya mtazamo wa dunia, falsafa, mawazo, maoni na tafakari. Inahifadhi maswali, mawazo yanayoendelea na misimamo iliyofikiriwa bila kujifanya kuwa yote ni aina moja ya kauli.

06 / IKAI · MIRROR

A mirror,
not a judge.

RESEARCH · WORK IN PROGRESS

Kioo,
si hakimu.

UTAFITI · UNAENDELEA

IKAI investigates reflective computing: the integration of reflective optics, sensing, displays, local computation and human-directed interaction in physical environments. “IKAI” is a Turkana female name meaning “home.”

The mirror is an initial experimental embodiment, not the limit of the research. Any future sensing or collection must be transparent, consent-based, purpose-limited and documented. IKAI must not identify, rank, diagnose, covertly monitor or secretly profile people.

IKAI huchunguza kompyuta tafakari: kuunganisha vioo, vihisi, skrini, kompyuta za ndani na mwingiliano unaoongozwa na binadamu katika mazingira halisi. “IKAI” ni jina la kike la Kiturkana linalomaanisha “nyumbani”.

Kioo ni mwanzo wa majaribio, si mpaka wa utafiti. Ukusanyaji wowote wa taarifa baadaye lazima uwe wazi, kwa ridhaa, kwa madhumuni maalumu na uandikwe. IKAI haitawatambua, kupanga viwango, kutoa utambuzi, kuwafuatilia kwa siri au kuwachunguza watu kisiri.

07 / GENJE · EXPRESSION

Rhythm made
embodied.

CREATIVE PRACTICE

Midundo inayodhihirika
kwa mwili.

SHUGHULI YA UBUNIFU

GENJE is a Genge-rooted creative practice where sound, code, rhythm, movement and the body meet. It explores livecoding, audiovisual experimentation, choreography, improvisation, movement notation and choreohacking.

Technology serves the creative work; it does not replace the human author, dancer or practitioner.

GENJE ni shughuli ya ubunifu yenye mizizi ya Genge, ambapo sauti, msimbo, midundo, mwendo na mwili hukutana. Inachunguza livecoding, majaribio ya sauti na picha, choreografia, ubunifu wa papo hapo, uandishi wa mwendo na choreohacking.

Teknolojia hutumikia kazi ya ubunifu; haichukui nafasi ya mwandishi, dansa au mtendaji binadamu.
