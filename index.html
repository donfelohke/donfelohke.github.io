IKAI · MIRROR · RESEARCH / WORK IN PROGRESS

A mirror, not a judge.

IKAI is reflective-computing research into optics, displays, local computation and human-directed interaction. Its name is a Turkana female name meaning “home” in the founder-provided naming context; independent linguistic and community validation remains a research question.

IKAI ni jina la kike la Kiturkana lenye maana ya “nyumbani” kulingana na maelezo ya mwanzilishi; jina hili linahusishwa na utafiti wa kompyuta tafakari kuhusu optiki, skrini, uchakataji wa ndani na mwingiliano unaoongozwa na binadamu.
