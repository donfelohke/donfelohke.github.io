# Content Governance

## Authority
`PUBLIC-FACTS-REGISTER.md` controls public factual claims. `SERVICE-CATALOG.md` controls the commercial service baseline. This document governs hierarchy, maturity labels and editorial treatment.

## Domain hierarchy
- **NAKEBUSEN / ROOT:** physical practice, loc care, craft and the only live commercial practice.
- **WARENA / CAPABILITY:** separate systems and infrastructure capability; not a NAKEBUSEN sub-brand or separate checkout.
- **IKAI / MIRROR:** research / WIP; not a released product.
- **GENJE / EXPRESSION:** creative practice; not a separate commercial division.
- **FELOH Corpus:** shared knowledge, documentation and provenance.
- **Don Feloh:** human author and accountable practitioner.

**ONLY NAKEBUSEN TRANSACTS.**

## Claim controls
Preserve approved prices, service labels, location and contacts. Do not invent availability, hours, street address, credentials, testimonials, outcomes or customer identities. Qualify provisional technical capabilities. Retain current copyright attribution pending its evidence control. Keep service payment and voluntary support distinct. Never ask for or publish a PIN, OTP or customer payment credential.

Any service or price change must reconcile the facts register, service catalogue, canonical copy and public page in one reviewed change set.
