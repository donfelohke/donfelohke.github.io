# Content Governance

## Authority

`PUBLIC-FACTS-REGISTER.md` controls public factual claims. `SERVICE-CATALOG.md` controls the commercial service baseline. This document governs hierarchy, maturity labels, editorial treatment and the relationship between the public domains.

## Public information architecture

- **NAKEBUSEN / ROOT:** first and only live commercial practice; natural loc care, services, approved published prices, location and direct enquiry.
- **WARENA / CAPABILITY:** separate systems and infrastructure capability domain; not a second live commercial brand, not a sub-brand and not a checkout.
- **Don Feloh:** human author, accountable practitioner, public professional identity and contact directory.
- **IKAI / MIRROR:** research / work in progress; not a released product.
- **GENJE / EXPRESSION:** creative practice; not a commercial service catalogue.
- **FELOH Corpus:** knowledge governance, documentation, research provenance and source repositories.
- **Shared footer — NAKEBUSEN · WARENA · DON FELOH:** business information, M-PESA, voluntary Support Us, GitHub and copyright.

**ONLY NAKEBUSEN TRANSACTS.**

## Maturity and claim controls

Preserve approved prices, service labels, the King's Square / Kisumu Road location wording and contacts. Do not invent availability, hours, street address, credentials, testimonials, outcomes or customer identities. Qualify provisional technical capabilities. IKAI must remain labelled research / WIP. GENJE must be described as creative practice. FELOH Corpus is the documentary record, not a product. Retain current copyright attribution pending its evidence control.

Keep NAKEBUSEN service payment and voluntary support distinct. Never ask for or publish a PIN, OTP or customer payment credential.

Any service or price change must reconcile the facts register, service catalogue, canonical copy and public page in one reviewed change set.
