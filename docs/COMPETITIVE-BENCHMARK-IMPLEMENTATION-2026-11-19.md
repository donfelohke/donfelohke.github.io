## Benchmark synthesis

The comparison covered specialist loc-care businesses, Kenyan IT support providers and research/creative-technology organisations. The implementation adopts transferable information patterns rather than copying competitors' visual identities or unverified commercial claims.

## Changes

### Homepage
- Added a three-step loc-care enquiry guide: service selection, useful condition/context, then direct agreement on suitability, availability, scope and price.
- Added a three-step WARENA engagement explanation: describe the problem/outcome, assess and agree scope, then complete and hand over the agreed work.
- Localised every new statement in the existing English/Kiswahili dictionary.
- Refined the visual system toward an editorial surface: removed decorative hero glow, reduced card ornamentation and retained restrained tactile feedback.
- Preserved the approved service catalogue, prices, booking boundary, payment details, support placement, navigation and commercial hierarchy.

### Research page
- Added a project-status vocabulary for LIVE, PROTOTYPE, RESEARCH and PROPOSAL.
- Clarified that status applies to an artefact or claim, not an entire research domain.
- Added an explicit rule against promoting unknown or weakly evidenced work to LIVE.
- Reduced decorative card treatment to improve reading hierarchy.

## Acceptance checks
- Homepage enquiry guide contains exactly three steps.
- Digital engagement process contains exactly three steps.
- Both new process blocks remain understandable in Kiswahili.
- Research page defines all four status labels and retains the commercial boundary.
- Existing service-price, M-PESA, WhatsApp, theme, language, navigation and responsive tests remain in force.

## Evidence limits
This implementation does not add testimonials, customer photographs, service durations, opening hours, street address, map pins, certifications, guaranteed availability, or new pricing. Those require evidence or owner confirmation. A successful CI run is not a substitute for real-device, assistive-technology or deployed-site review.
