# NAKEBUSEN Website Copy — Swahili-first master

**Status:** Proposed editorial source for review. This file documents recommended copy; it does not itself change the live website.  
**Language policy:** Kiswahili is the default. English is available through a persistent, accessible language toggle. Keep the visitor's selected language across pages where technically feasible, without requiring an account.  
**Enquiry guidance:** Clearly distinguish available services from research, concepts and work in progress. Explain that booking requests require direct confirmation.

## Editorial principles

- Lead with the visitor's need and the service offered; explain the wider ecosystem after that.
- Use plain, natural Kiswahili first. Avoid literal translations that sound unnatural.
- Keep prices, location, booking terms and contact routes visible.
- A booking request is not a confirmed appointment.
- Do not imply that a technical service, product, partnership, result or credential is available unless verified.
- No graphics are required to understand the page. Text links to the separate Gallery repository may be used where relevant.
- Keep claims specific and evidence-backed; describe IKAI as work in progress.

## 5. WARENA — the technical approach

### Uhuru ni mfumo

WARENA ni kazi ya kujenga na kuwezesha mifumo ya kompyuta inayoweza kukaguliwa, kutunzwa na kujengwa upya. Tunapendelea zana huria, nyaraka zilizo wazi na mifumo inayowapa watu uwezo wa kuelewa teknolojia wanayotegemea.

**English**

WARENA develops and enables computing systems that can be inspected, maintained and reproduced. The approach favours libre tools, clear documentation and systems that help people understand the technology they depend on.
