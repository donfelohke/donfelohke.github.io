## Commercial rule

**ONLY NAKEBUSEN TRANSACTS.**

- LOCS and DIGITAL are the two customer-facing commercial paths.
- GENJE ZA WARENA is governing intention, not a separate business unit.
- WARENA Systems is the technical identity within DIGITAL, not a separate checkout.
- IKAI is research, not a released product.

## Non-negotiables

1. Human authority remains central.
2. Cultural heritage and technical ingenuity belong together.
3. Public claims must be evidence-led.
4. Starting prices must match the service catalogue.
5. An enquiry is not a confirmed booking.
6. No braiding or shaving is advertised.
7. Do not imply unverified availability, street address or opening hours.
8. Do not describe research or proposals as released products.
9. Preserve the exact brand lockup.
10. Distinguish specification from implementation and verification.

## Adopted language

- GENJE ZA WARENA is adopted as “THE FIRE OF THE FORMIDABLE”; this is a brand interpretation, not verified etymology.
