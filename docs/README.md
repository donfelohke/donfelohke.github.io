## Publication rules

- NAKEBUSEN is the primary commercial entry and the only transaction interface.
- An enquiry does not confirm a booking.
- Use the Public Facts Register and Service Catalogue for current public facts; do not infer from historical briefs.
- Research documents are not proof of implementation, deployment, certification or product release.
- Keep the page static, text-first, accessible, bilingual and free of tracking or unnecessary external runtime dependencies.
- Preserve manual payment instructions and verification safeguards; do not introduce API, STK push or automated payment-success claims.
- Retain historical records for provenance and mark superseded material rather than silently deleting it.
