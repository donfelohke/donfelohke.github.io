## Purpose

This note connects the public hub to the research programme without turning the commercial website into a research archive or changing its current information architecture.

## Publication boundary

- The FELOH Corpus is the source of record for historical research, claims, citations, evidence labels and corrections.
- The public website may provide a concise, human-reviewed pointer to the research when explicitly approved.
- Do not copy preliminary claims, unverified statistics, archaeological interpretations or speculative futures into public copy without claim-level review.
- Do not add maps, photographs, artefact images or community imagery without Gallery provenance, rights, consent and approval.
- Do not change NAKEBUSEN prices, service terms, payment details, enquiry language, navigation, language toggles, theme controls or site layout as part of this research update.
- Keep public website content text-first and consistent with existing publication and accessibility standards.
- A repository commit is not a deployment. Verify Pages publication separately before describing any public change.

## Source hierarchy

1. FELOH Corpus master dossier and source register.
2. Specialist domain research in WARENA, IKAI, GENJE and NAKEBUSEN.
3. Gallery for visual and documentary asset rights.
4. Official datasets and primary documents for current statistics and legal claims.
