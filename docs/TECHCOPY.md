## 1. Positioning

**Practical computing. Sovereign systems.**

NAKEBUSEN DIGITAL provides practical computing and infrastructure services under the WARENA technical identity.

The work should help people and organisations understand, maintain and adapt their systems, including in environments where connectivity is unreliable.

Do not claim that a system is secure, sovereign, reproducible or fully local-first merely because it uses open-source software. Claims must match the actual design and verification evidence.

## 2. Public capability areas

1. **GNU/Linux** — installation, administration and troubleshooting.
2. **Workstations** — diagnostics, recovery and system maintenance.
3. **Networks** — Wi-Fi, LAN and router configuration.
4. **Infrastructure** — small-office servers and shared services.
5. **Learning spaces** — laboratory, classroom and cybercafé systems.
6. **Local-first** — offline-capable environments where appropriate.
7. **Development** — reproducible toolchains and systems engineering practice.

These are scoped capability areas. Verify availability, hardware support, feasibility, location and schedule before accepting an engagement.

## 3. Enquiry and delivery

Ask the customer for:

- Existing setup and environment.
- Problem and desired outcome.
- Location and timeline.
- Hardware and software constraints.
- Budget and support expectations.
- Connectivity, offline and multi-user requirements.

Before work begins, agree scope, deliverables, assumptions, exclusions, price and support terms. An enquiry is not an accepted project.

## 4. Technical principles

- Human authority and explicit consent.
- Local-first where it provides a practical benefit.
- Reproducibility supported by evidence.
- Maintainability and understandable operations.
- Interoperability and open standards.
- Security by design, without unsupported guarantees.
- Observability and documented handover.
- Clear separation between design, implementation, testing and verification.

## 5. GNU/Linux and Guix language

Distinguish a distribution release from a channel commit. Distinguish a configuration from a successful build, a successful build from a tested deployment, and a tested deployment from a verified reproducible result.

Do not claim reproducibility without recording the source revision, build inputs, environment and verification method.

## 6. IKAI Mirror

IKAI is research into reflective, local-first computing.

Principles:

- Human Remains Author.
- Mirror ≠ Judge.
- Local First.

Conceptual modes include MIRROR, AWARE, REFLECT, PRACTICE, GENJE and SYSTEM. These are not claims of implemented features. Mark maturity accurately: Concept, Proposed, Prototype, Implemented, Tested, Verified, Released or Retired.

IKAI is not a released commercial product and must not have a purchase or checkout CTA.

## 7. Forbidden claims

- Guaranteed uptime without contract evidence.
- Security guarantees beyond demonstrated practice.
- Reproducibility without a repeatable verification record.
- Enterprise-scale capacity without evidence.
- Client names or case studies without permission.
- IKAI as a shipped product.
- WARENA as an independent commercial checkout brand.
