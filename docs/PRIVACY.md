# Privacy and data minimisation

The site is static and should work without accounts, analytics, cookies, external fonts or third-party JavaScript.

The site publishes business contact details supplied for public use. Clicking WhatsApp leaves this site and is subject to that service's policies. Do not add forms or customer databases without a documented need and privacy review.

Avoid tracking pixels, fingerprinting, covert profiling, face identification, diagnosis or scoring under IKAI, and committing customer details, chat transcripts, appointment records or secrets. Do not claim no data is processed anywhere; hosting and external services may process ordinary connection data.

Any proposed forms, cookies, mailing lists, payments, accounts, analytics or APIs require a purpose, data-flow description, retention policy, security review and explicit owner approval. Collect the minimum necessary and explain it plainly.
