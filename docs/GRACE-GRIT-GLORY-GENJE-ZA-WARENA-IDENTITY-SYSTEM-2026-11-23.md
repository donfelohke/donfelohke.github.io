## Website-specific decisions

- The page is the public home for DON FELOH / @donfelohke.
- The hero leads with GRACE · GRIT · GLORY and the signature GENJE ZA WARENA | NAKEBUSEN.
- The commercial entry point remains NAKEBUSEN LOCS in Eldoret.
- WARENA, GENJE and IKAI remain distinct technical, creative and research domains.
- Only NAKEBUSEN transacts; the enquiry-not-booking disclosure remains visible.
- Preserve the native, text-first HTML/CSS implementation, EN default / Kiswahili toggle, service cards, contact routes, location, payment safeguards, support footer and three-item mobile dock.

## Visual direction

Liquid Glass Terminal × Editorial Hybrid. Forest-green technical surfaces, ivory editorial text, gold/ochre heritage accents, terracotta expressive accents, Cormorant Garamond / Inter / JetBrains Mono. Glass depth must not compromise contrast or reduced-transparency support.

## Release gates

- Run existing automated tests.
- Check semantic structure, language switching, links, prices and booking/payment disclosures.
- Check mobile and desktop layouts, keyboard focus, reduced motion and reduced transparency.
- Perform manual browser and screen-reader review.
- Verify GitHub Pages deployment separately; source changes do not prove publication.
