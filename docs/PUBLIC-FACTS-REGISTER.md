## Evidence classes

- **F1 — Founder-supplied operational fact:** supplied by the operator; not necessarily independently verified.
- **F2 — Approved brand decision:** explicitly approved identity or copy.
- **F3 — Provisional capability:** publish only with an accurate scope or availability qualifier.
- **F4 — Requires verification:** do not present as independently established.
- **F5 — Prohibited claim:** do not publish.
