01 / TECHNICAL CONSTITUTION

Ownership. Freedom. Reproducibility.

WARENA is libre-first, not GNU-exclusive. GNU is foundational, while technically and ethically suitable free-software projects from other ecosystems remain eligible for evaluation. Reuse, adapt, interoperate and credit existing work before proposing a new invention.

  • Local-first and user-controlled operation.
  • Free software, source availability and repairability.
  • Reproducible builds and inspectable configuration.
  • No telemetry, DRM, tivoization or unnecessary cloud dependency.
  • Human-directed automation, with explicit permissions and clear limits.

03 / MATURITY AND EVIDENCE

Claims follow evidence.

WARENA distinguishes what is available from what has been demonstrated, researched or merely proposed. A repository, design note or configuration is not proof of a deployed or independently reproducible system.

AVAILABLE means a service or artefact is actually accessible. DEMONSTRATED means an observed demonstration exists. RESEARCH means an investigated topic. WIP means active but unfinished work. PROPOSED means a candidate direction.

Technical enquiries are routed through NAKEBUSEN. Scope, feasibility, availability, schedule and terms must be agreed directly. WARENA is not a separate commercial checkout.
