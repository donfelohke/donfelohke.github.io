# AI-Assisted Changes and Website Source Provenance

**Status:** Proposed contributor policy  
**Canonical policy:** [FELOH Corpus — AI-Assisted Development and Source Integrity](https://github.com/donfelohke/feloh-corpus/blob/main/AI-ASSISTED-DEVELOPMENT.md)

This repository applies the canonical policy to static website code, public copy, accessibility, metadata, tests and build/deployment workflows.

## Rules

- Do not submit customer records, private messages, payment references, credentials, secrets or private business data to external AI tools.
- Do not use AI-generated testimonials, reviews, credentials, service guarantees or unsupported business claims.
- Human review is required for all material public copy, prices, service descriptions, payment instructions and privacy statements.
- Material AI assistance in substantive code, copy, assets or research must be disclosed in the PR when it affects authorship, rights or factual reliability.
- Never use an LLM to rewrite unauthorised or unreleased source to conceal its origin.
- Preserve third-party licence and attribution notices; the existing GPL-3.0 repository licence is unchanged.
- Website code licensing does not grant rights to customer photographs, Gallery assets, fonts, logos or third-party media.
- Keep the site static, accessible, dependency-light and consistent with the text-first asset boundary.

## Verification

Record commands, browser/device scope, accessibility checks, workflow results and known limitations. A merged PR does not prove deployment; verify the published site separately before claiming release.
