# Governance and stewardship

Purpose: make the connected practice legible and transferable rather than dependent on permanent founder control.

## Roles
- Human author/maintainer: confirms direction and public claims.
- Contributors: propose documented changes and run checks.
- Reviewers: assess correctness, privacy, accessibility, maintainability and evidence.
- Automation: performs repeatable checks; it does not grant itself authority or prove product claims.

## Principles
1. Preserve human authorship and attribution.
2. Prefer transparent, reversible decisions.
3. Protect customer dignity and personal data.
4. Keep transactions within NAKEBUSEN.
5. Do not present IKAI as a judge or identity system.
6. Record consequential decisions in pull requests or dated records.
7. Support succession and distributed stewardship through documentation and least-privilege access.

Use individual accounts, two-factor authentication and least privilege. Never share passwords or tokens. Review access periodically and record stewardship transfers explicitly.
