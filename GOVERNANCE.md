# Governance and stewardship

Purpose: make the connected practice legible and transferable rather than dependent on permanent founder control.

Human author/maintainer confirms direction and public claims. Contributors propose documented changes and run checks. Reviewers assess correctness, privacy, accessibility, maintainability and evidence. Automation performs repeatable checks but does not grant itself authority or prove product claims.

Principles: preserve authorship; prefer transparent and reversible decisions; protect customer dignity and data; keep transactions within NAKEBUSEN; do not present IKAI as a judge or identity system; record consequential decisions in pull requests; support succession through documentation and least-privilege access.

Use individual accounts, two-factor authentication and least privilege. Never share passwords or tokens. Review access periodically and record stewardship transfers explicitly.
