00 / ORIENTATION

Not four separate identities.

The practices are distinct, but connected by authorship, curiosity and a commitment to work that can be understood, examined and carried forward.

NAKEBUSEN grounds the work in beauty, heritage and craft. WARENA develops technical capability. IKAI investigates reflective computing and embodied environments. GENJE explores sound, movement and creative computation. FELOH Corpus preserves shared knowledge, decisions and provenance.

FELOH generates. NAKEBUSEN grounds. IKAI reflects. WARENA enables. GENJE embodies.

04 / HOW TO READ THIS ATLAS

Documentation is not deployment.

LIVE

Current public practice or service, with its own terms and boundaries.

RESEARCH

A question, investigation or synthesis. Not a released product.

PROPOSAL

A candidate architecture or experiment. Not an implementation claim.

SOURCE

The canonical repository or document where the detailed record lives.

A document on a repository branch establishes that the document exists. It does not, by itself, prove a system is built, tested, deployed or validated. The human remains the author.
