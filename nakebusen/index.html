02 / ABOUT · ORIGIN AND PURPOSE

Beautiful roots. Sovereign futures.

NAKEBUSEN is a Turkana name meaning “beautiful”. Its origin carries a connection to where we started—and to the futures we can shape without abandoning memory, identity or dignity.

NAKEBUSEN was established around three convictions: Black Beauty, Digital Sovereignty and Creative Expression. Its own practice is grounded in beauty: recognising, caring for and expressing ourselves as we are, while making room for a broader Black psyche.

Beauty is not something imposed from outside. It can be recognised, reclaimed, cultivated and expressed. Our return to origin is not a rejection of the future; it is a way of building that future with memory, dignity and a stronger sense of ourselves.

NAKEBUSEN is the beauty and craft practice within a wider body of work authored by Don Feloh. Its commercial purpose remains distinct and direct.
