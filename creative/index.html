01 / GENJE · SOUND + MOVEMENT

Rhythm made embodied.

CREATIVE PRACTICE · RESEARCH + WORKS

GENJE is a Genge-rooted creative practice where music, code, rhythm, movement and the body meet. It explores Genge, electronic African club music, neo-soul, reggae and dancehall influences, algorithmic composition, livecoding and performance.

Its practice extends into choreography, improvisation, choreohacking, movement notation and embodied knowledge. Technology serves the creative work; it does not replace the human author, dancer or practitioner.

Practice areas

  • Genge, electronic music and authored sonic experiments.
  • Algorithmic composition and livecoding.
  • Dance, choreography, improvisation and movement studies.
  • Choreohacking and relationships between movement, sound and code.
  • Performance documentation, rights, attribution and provenance.

Works and experiments are labelled according to evidence: LIVE, PROTOTYPE, RESEARCH, CONCEPT, HISTORICAL or UNKNOWN.

02 / IKAI · REFLECTION + HOME

A mirror, not a judge.

RESEARCH · WORK IN PROGRESS · NOT A RELEASED PRODUCT

IKAI is a Turkana female name meaning “home.” It investigates reflective computing: the integration of reflective optics, sensing, displays, local computation and human-directed interaction in physical environments.

The mirror is an initial experimental embodiment, not the limit of the research. Directions include two-way mirror displays, transparent displays, digital and virtual mirrors, spatial interfaces and real-time bidirectional input/output.

IKAI supports reflection and exploration. It must not identify, rank, diagnose, covertly monitor or secretly profile people. Any future collection or sensing must be transparent, consent-based, purpose-limited and documented.

IKAI remains research and work in progress. No product release, validated capability or commercial service is claimed here.
